**Version:** 2.0

## 1. Goal

Position Dirga Halim Susilo as a job-ready **Data Analyst and Business Intelligence candidate** who can:

- translate business needs into analytical questions;
- clean, transform, and explore data;
- design decision-ready dashboards and KPI reports;
- communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders;
- work comfortably across Excel, SQL, Python, BI tools, and production data systems.

Full-stack and AI work remains supporting evidence of technical breadth. It must not compete with the primary Data Analyst position.

## 5. Case Study Standard

Every featured analytics case study must contain:

- the business question;
- a concise problem and deliverable description;
- visible dashboard evidence;
- a small number of verified dataset metrics;
- tools used;
- a repository link.

Avoid unsupported claims such as estimated time savings, business impact, user counts, or revenue unless a source or measurement method is available.

## 6. Content Rules

- Use professional English consistently.
- Prioritize analysis and decision support over tool lists.
- Use numbers only when they can be verified from the dashboard, dataset, repository, or source system.
- Keep project descriptions concise and outcome-oriented.
- Maintain the same email, phone number, domain, role, and timeline across the website and resume.
- Avoid presenting software engineering, AI, and data analysis as three equal career targets.

## 7. Resume Requirements

- One-page A4 layout.
- ATS-readable text with no profile photo, icons, or complex graphics.
- Data Analyst title and summary.
- Core skills near the top.
- Three most relevant data roles.
- Three visual analytics projects.
- Consistent email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, and website.
- Clickable contact links.

## 8. Quality Requirements

- Responsive from mobile to large desktop.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states.
- Light and dark modes with readable contrast.
- Optimized local WebP dashboard previews.
- Valid metadata, JSON-LD, sitemap, robots, and Open Graph image.
- `npm run lint` and `npm run build` must pass before publication.
- Resume must be rendered to PNG and visually inspected before replacement.
